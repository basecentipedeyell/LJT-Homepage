# Junteng Liu

## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the **HKUST NLP Group** at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, advised by **Prof. Junxian He**. Prof. He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I graduated from SJTU in June 2024 with a B.Eng.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. My research interests include:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, 2024–Present.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2020–2024 (graduated in June 2024).
